AIntro

Hello, I’m Arda,a creative developer.

I’m a computer engineer. I work on everything between how a product looks on screen and how it sits on the table.

Creative developerScroll down

I don’t fit in one field and I don’t hide it. In the morning I write the backend patch that fixes a check-in race condition, at noon I render a machine’s vinyl wrap in Blender, in the evening I tune the drag-and-drop feel of my own iOS app. The pieces look separate but the job is the same: making one thing work end to end.
